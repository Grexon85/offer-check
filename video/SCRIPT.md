## Saying the figures

- £82,000: "eighty-two thousand pounds"
- 3%: "three percent"; 10% and 20%: "ten and twenty percent"
- £408: "four hundred and eight pounds"
- £28 to £31: "twenty-eight pounds to thirty-one"
- £86,900: "eighty-six thousand, nine hundred pounds"

## The example on screen

Today: £70,000, 10% employer pension, 5% paid in by salary sacrifice, 10% bonus, £2,400 and 7.5 hours a week commuting.
Offer: £82,000, 3% pension plus a 3% match (not salary sacrifice), 8% target bonus counted at half, £600 medical, £1,500 and 5 hours commuting, £5,000 sign-on repayable within 12 months. Plan 2 student loan, England.
Results (2026/27): break-even, 5.2% better after tax (9.2% before tax), take-home up £408 a month, hourly rate £28.06 → £31.31, year one +£5,542, base needed for Consider £86,884.
