How this is worked out

Total value = base + employer pension + the part of the bonus you can count on + allowances + medical. The offer also gains or loses the value of any holiday difference (a day's pay per day) and any change in commute cost. Commute time is shown but not given a cash value.

After tax takes off 2026/27 income tax and employee National Insurance from salary, bonus, allowances and holiday value, and income tax from medical cover (a taxable benefit). Employer pension is counted in full because it's paid in before tax, though you'll pay some tax when you draw it. Commute costs come from taxed pay, so they count in full.

Not included: student loan repayments, your own pension contributions or salary sacrifice, other income, and non-standard tax codes. Figures are illustrative estimates, not financial or tax advice.
