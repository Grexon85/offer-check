How this is worked out

Total value = base + employer pension (core plus any match) + the part of the bonus you can count on + allowances + medical. The offer also gains or loses the value of any holiday difference (a day's pay per day) and any change in commute cost. Commute time is given a cash value only if you set what an hour of your time is worth in step 3. Holiday is compared in weeks off, so a four-day week's 20 days equals a five-day week's 25.

Hourly rate, all in = each package after tax, less its commute cost, divided by contracted plus commute hours over the weeks you work (after holiday and 8 bank holidays).

After tax takes off 2026/27 income tax, employee National Insurance and any student loan from salary, bonus, allowances and holiday value, and income tax from medical cover (a taxable benefit). Your own pension contribution gets income tax relief, and also saves National Insurance if it's by salary sacrifice; it counts in full as value because it's still your money. Employer pension is counted in full because it's paid in before tax, though you'll pay some tax when you draw it. Commute costs come from taxed pay, so they count in full.

Monthly take-home is regular pay (salary and allowances) after tax, National Insurance, student loan and your pension, before any bonus.

Not included: other income, non-standard tax codes, bonus sacrifice, and tax you'll pay when you draw your pension. Figures are illustrative estimates, not financial or tax advice.
