Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: what you type into Offer Check stays on your own device. We don't ask you to sign up, we don't collect your figures, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

1. Who we are

Offer Check is operated by the Offer Check team. For the limited personal data described below, we are the data controller under UK data protection law.

2. What you enter stays on your device

Your salary, pension, bonus, benefits, commute details and recruiter notes are saved only in your own browser's local storage, so they're still there when you come back. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time with Clear all in the tool, or by clearing your browser's site data.

3. What our hosting provider sees

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. As with any website, when you visit, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This is kept in short-lived logs and is not used to identify you or build a profile of you. We rely on our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

4. Cookies

We don't set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may set a strictly necessary security cookie to protect the site from abuse.

5. Third parties

We don't sell or share personal data. Fonts and all other files are served from our own site, so your browser doesn't contact other companies when you use Offer Check.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data, including to access, correct or erase it. Because your figures never leave your device, you control them directly. If you have a concern about how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Changes

If how we handle information changes, we'll update this notice and the date at the top.
